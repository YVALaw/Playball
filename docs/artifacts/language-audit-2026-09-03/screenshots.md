# Language audit: rendered evidence

Captured from the current source in the Codex in-app browser at an isolated local origin. These are original screenshots from this audit, not redesigned mockups. The viewport was 1280 × 720; some views use the game's narrow app frame and some overlays expand to the viewport. Only visible portions are evidenced; offscreen and untriggered states are covered separately by source review. No layout redesign or full accessibility test was performed.

## 1. Coach creation — mostly clear

**Keep:** Meet the coach, Coach name, basic hair choices. **Change:** “How he shows up” → “Your look”; “Where he is from” → “Home state.” Give color controls human-readable accessible names instead of hex values. The source and accessibility tree confirm the hex naming.

![Step 1: coach creation](C:/Users/cronu/OneDrive/Desktop/G/playball/docs/artifacts/language-audit-2026-09-03/01-coach.jpg)

## 2. Play mode — understandable, too metaphorical

“Set your desk” does not say what this choice changes. Use “How hands-on?” with consistent **Full control / Casual** choices. The list of responsibilities already explains the difference; the “how much reaches you” kicker adds work.

![Step 2: play mode](C:/Users/cronu/OneDrive/Desktop/G/playball/docs/artifacts/language-audit-2026-09-03/02-play-style.jpg)

## 3. Interview — clear choices, repetitive voice

This scenario is concrete and worth keeping. **Question 1 of 5** is clearer than **5 QUESTIONS · 1**. Preserve different coaching decisions and skill effects, but loosen the formal “He… He… He…” rhythm across the pool. A short line like “He sits. I'll tell him myself.” sounds spoken.

![Step 3: interview](C:/Users/cronu/OneDrive/Desktop/G/playball/docs/artifacts/language-audit-2026-09-03/03-interview.jpg)

## 4. Game plan — good names, padded descriptions

Small ball, Power, Pitching and defense, Balanced are recognizable. “Wears the quiet nights” and “takes what the game offers” add tone without clarifying the setting. Replace the blurbs with concrete tendencies and costs; keep the visible tactic summary.

![Step 4: game plan](C:/Users/cronu/OneDrive/Desktop/G/playball/docs/artifacts/language-audit-2026-09-03/04-strategy.jpg)

## 5. Offers — largely successful

School, conference, stars, roster strength, contract length and challenge are easy to compare. This needs small terminology/number-label edits, not a new joke. “Programs that called” works.

![Step 5: offers list](C:/Users/cronu/OneDrive/Desktop/G/playball/docs/artifacts/language-audit-2026-09-03/05-offers.jpg)

## 6. Offer detail — too much narration

“This roster is a window, and windows close,” then “Nobody arrives finished. Everybody leaves better or leaves early,” then another interpretation of the school creates a stack of speeches. Keep the school-specific identity and concrete goal. Use “The roster's better than the reputation. Make a name for them.” only when the underlying roster assessment supports it. Label the win target explicitly.

![Step 6: offer detail](C:/Users/cronu/OneDrive/Desktop/G/playball/docs/artifacts/language-audit-2026-09-03/06-offer-detail.jpg)

## 7. Home and tutorial — strong actions, verbose help

**Set lineup / Play ball / Sim game / Sim week** are good. The tutorial repeats them with a coffee joke and extra narration. The captain card makes a simple vacancy into a promise about the whole season. Draft: **Pick a captain. 3 players are eligible.** Keep actual effects in help.

![Step 7: home tutorial](C:/Users/cronu/OneDrive/Desktop/G/playball/docs/artifacts/language-audit-2026-09-03/07-home-tutorial.jpg)

## 8. Settings — functional labels, overexplained ownership

Settings destinations are clear. The note about settings following dynasties and riding saves should become one accurate sentence about browser-level preferences and per-dynasty controls. No character performance is needed here.

![Step 8: settings](C:/Users/cronu/OneDrive/Desktop/G/playball/docs/artifacts/language-audit-2026-09-03/08-settings.jpg)

## 9. Sound — routine controls should sound routine

“The crack, the glove, and a crowd that knows the score” → **Game sounds and crowd noise.** “Haptics” → **Vibration**; helper **Vibrate during key plays.** Keep ordinary switches easy to understand.

![Step 9: sound settings](C:/Users/cronu/OneDrive/Desktop/G/playball/docs/artifacts/language-audit-2026-09-03/09-sound.jpg)

## 10. Saves — shorten explanation, protect the warning

**Save a copy** and **Load** work. Draft helper: **Save a copy of your dynasty to return to later.** Draft new-dynasty warning: **Starting a new dynasty replaces the autosave. Save a copy to keep this career.** Lower content continues offscreen in this viewport; its confirmations were source-reviewed, not executed. The save-name field should have an explicit accessible label.
