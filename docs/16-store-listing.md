## Full description (4000)

```
You are the head coach. Ninety-six programs, eight conferences, a forty-five game season, and a June that ends in Omaha. Playball is a college baseball dynasty game built for the phone, offline, with no ads and no account.

MANAGE EVERY PITCH — OR NONE
Sit in the dugout and call the game at bat by at bat: steal, bunt, hit-and-run, go to the bullpen, visit the mound, position your defense. Or set your strategy, hand the game to your staff, and simulate a day, a week, or the whole season. Either way the same engine plays it, calibrated to the modern NCAA Division I game.

RECRUIT ALL SEASON
Twelve weeks of recruiting run alongside the schedule. Nine things a recruit weighs, a pitch and a major move a week, binding promises that follow him onto your roster, and ninety-five rival programs chasing the same class. A recruiting coordinator builds real pipelines in the states you work.

BUILD A PROGRAM
Hire a hitting coach, a pitching coach and a recruiting coordinator. Give each a standing directive and a multi-week project — a contact block, a velocity lab, a pipeline — powered by the facilities you fund. Upgrade the cage, the pen and the clubhouse. Spend coaching points on yourself.

SURVIVE THE OFFSEASON
Awards night. The board's verdict on your season, and your contract. The MLB draft trying to take your junior — argue him out of it. The transfer portal in both directions. Signing day. Then February again.

A WORLD THAT MOVES
Rival programs are run by ninety-five named coaches with careers of their own. The pecking order changes over decades. Conferences realign. Coaches get poached. Records fall and go into a book that remembers every season you have ever played.

GOD MODE (optional purchase)
Rewrite the world: edit any player, program, coach, staff, budget, league or schedule. Fork any career into a sandbox and keep the original honest.

• Full 3D ballpark with a camera that follows the play, and a 2D field for older phones
• Injuries, morale, redshirts, academic eligibility, two-way players
• Double-elimination conference tournaments, regionals, and the national championship
• Sound, haptics, and ninety-six procedurally generated crests
• Works completely offline. Your saves never leave your phone.
```
