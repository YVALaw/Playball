`scripts/prep-sfx.mjs`. Every licence was verified against the sound's
freesound page on **September 16 2026** ahead of the store release. None is
NonCommercial, so all eight ship cleanly with the paid god-mode IAP. The
three Attribution (CC-BY) samples require the author credited in the shipped
app; that credit rides the build on **Settings → Sound → Sound credits**
(`src/ui/screens/Settings.tsx`, `SOUND_CREDITS`). The CC0 samples require
nothing but are credited there too.
